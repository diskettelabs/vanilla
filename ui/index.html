welcome to vanilla.

A clean, fast interface for your local and cloud AI models.

1
Choose a model
Click the model name at the top-left to pick from all available providers — Ollama, OpenAI, Anthropic, and more.
2
Start a conversation
Type in the bar at the bottom and press Enter. Use Shift+Enter for newlines. Click the input to expand and reveal attachment and tool options.
3
Navigate your history
All conversations are saved automatically in the sidebar. Search across all of them instantly with ⌘K.
