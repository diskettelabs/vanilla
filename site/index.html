Features

Everything you need for local AI

Vanilla Chat runs entirely on your machine. No data ever leaves your computer.

⚡

Real-time Streaming

Responses appear token-by-token via Server-Sent Events for a smooth, natural conversation feel.

💬

Conversations

Create, switch between, and delete chat sessions. Your entire history is saved locally as Markdown.

🚀

Model Selection

Pick any model available in your local Ollama instance, right from the UI.

🍦

14 Ice Cream Themes

Vanilla, Chocolate, Strawberry, Mint, Lemon, Lime, Peach, Plum, Raspberry, Lavender, Dragonfruit, Dreamsicle, Blue Moon, and Monochrome.

🔒

100% Private

All data stays local. Nothing leaves your machine. Connect to Ollama running on localhost.

🔧

File Uploads

Attach images and documents to your conversations. Images are automatically compressed.
