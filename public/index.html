AI that feels like yours—fast, private, and ridiculously useful.

First things first: what should I call you?

We'll use your name for greetings and the occasional personalized prompt. No pressure—you can always change it later.

Where's your AI running?

Vanilla works with local models (think: private, offline, zero latency) or cloud providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, and friends). Pick what fits your vibe.

Want your chats named automatically?

Vanilla can generate smart titles for your conversations using a tiny local model (about 350 MB via Ollama). It's quick, private, and runs entirely on your machine. Or skip it—you can always rename chats manually.

Almost there. Let's check your Ollama setup.

Vanilla connects to Ollama at localhost:11434 by default. Only tweak this if you're running Ollama on a custom host or port. (If you're not sure, leave it alone—it's probably right.)

One last thing: your API key.

Pick your cloud provider and drop in your API key. Don't worry—it's stored locally in your browser and never leaves your machine. You can swap providers or update keys anytime in Settings.
